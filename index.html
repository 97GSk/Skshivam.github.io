About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher working in the field of ultracold atomic gases, with a primary focus on Bose–Einstein condensates and nonlinear quantum systems.

My research involves theoretical and computational studies of nonlinear quantum fluids using the Gross–Pitaevskii equation (GPE) and the extended Gross–Pitaevskii equation (eGPE). Apart from this, I lead the mechanics and optics lab for B.Tech students in biotechnology and computer science, guiding them through fundamental concepts and hands-on experiments. This experience has allowed me to blend my passion for quantum mechanics with practical instruction, fostering curiosity and critical thinking among students.

I am particularly interested in understanding how competing interactions give rise to different quantum states, collective phenomena and phase transitions in ultracold atomic systems.

My Research

A significant part of my research combines analytical approaches with numerical simulations to investigate nonlinear quantum phenomena and the emergence of different many-body states in ultracold atomic systems.

01

Surface Tension in Quantum Liquids

I have studied the surface tension of quantum liquids by considering different interaction contributions, including mean-field (MF), beyond-mean-field (BMF) and three-body interactions.

Both quasi-one-dimensional (Q1D) and one-dimensional (1D) systems are investigated to understand the role of competing interactions in the formation and stabilization of quantum-droplet-like states.

MF BMF 3-Body Q1D 1D
02

Josephson Oscillation & Self-Trapping

Another part of my research focuses on the nonlinear dynamics of quantum liquids in a double-well configuration.

In particular, I investigate the transition between Josephson oscillation (JO) and nonlinear self-trapping (ST) using an extended two-mode description.

The resulting dynamics are compared with the excitation spectrum obtained from Bogoliubov analysis.

Josephson Dynamics Self-Trapping Two-Mode Model Bogoliubov
03

Dipolar Interactions & Density Modulation

I am also investigating the effects of dipole–dipole interactions (DDI) on quantum-liquid states and fragmentation.

The influence of dipolar interaction strength, beyond-mean-field effects, particle number and coupling parameters is studied to understand the emergence and structure of fragmented density distributions.

DDI Fragmentation BMF Quantum Liquids

Information Measures & Fidelity Susceptibility

I am exploring information-theoretic and quantum-correlation measures to characterize changes between localized, soliton-like and flat-top quantum states.

The analysis includes quantities such as Shannon entropy, Fisher information, fidelity susceptibility, Wigner distributions.

Shannon Fisher Wigner Fidelity Susceptibility
