## Research

### 1. Surface Tension in Quantum Liquids

I have studied the surface tension of quantum liquids using different interaction contributions, including:

- Mean-field two-body interactions
- Beyond-mean-field (BMF/LHY) corrections
- Three-body interactions

The study considers both quasi-one-dimensional and one-dimensional quantum systems and investigates how different interaction terms influence the surface properties of quantum droplets.

**Publication:**

> S. S. Adusumalli, K. Senapati, S. Singh, A. Khan,  
> *Physics Letters A* **516**, 129638.

---

### 2. Josephson Oscillation and Nonlinear Self-Trapping

Another part of my research focuses on the nonlinear dynamics of quantum liquids in a double-well configuration.

In particular, I investigate the transition between:

**Josephson Oscillation (JO)**  
↓  
**Nonlinear Self-Trapping (ST)**

The dynamics are studied using an extended two-mode description and compared with the excitation spectrum obtained from the Bogoliubov analysis.

**Preprint:**

> S. Singh, S. Rashid, A. Khan,  
> *Josephson Oscillation and Nonlinear Self-Trapping in Quasi-one-dimensional Quantum Liquid.*

[Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00674)

---

### 3. Dipolar Interactions and Fragmentation

I am also investigating the effects of **dipole-dipole interactions (DDI)** on quantum-liquid states and fragmentation.

The role of:

- Dipolar interaction strength
- Beyond-mean-field interaction
- Particle number
- Coupling parameters

is studied to understand the emergence and structure of fragmented density distributions.

## Information-Theoretic Approach

I am exploring information-theoretic and quantum-correlation-based quantities to characterize changes in quantum states.

The quantities of interest include:

### Shannon Entropy

\[
S_x=-\int \rho(x)\ln[\rho(x)]\,dx
\]

### Fisher Information

\[
F_x=\int \frac{1}{\rho(x)}
\left(\frac{d\rho(x)}{dx}\right)^2 dx
\]

### Rényi Entropy

\[
R_\alpha =
\frac{1}{1-\alpha}
\ln\left(\int \rho(x)^\alpha dx\right)
\]

### Tsallis Entropy

\[
T_q =
\frac{1}{q-1}
\left(1-\int \rho(x)^q dx\right)
\]

I am also studying **Wigner functions, one-body density matrices, condensate fraction, pair correlations, and entanglement-related quantities** to investigate structural changes across soliton-to-droplet transitions.
